We are inviting submissions to the special issue "Urban System and Spatial Restructuring from the Perspective of Network Connections" in *Applied Spatial Analysis and Policy*.

The issue focuses on how urban systems and spatial structures are being reshaped through network connections and multidimensional flows, especially in the contexts of urban-rural integration, metropolitan development, and coordinated regional growth.

More information is available at the [special issue page](https://link.springer.com/collections/jicbecfhbh).
