I am serving as a **Guest Editor** for the special issue "Urban System and Spatial Restructuring from the Perspective of Network Connections" in *Applied Spatial Analysis and Policy*.

The issue focuses on how urban systems and spatial structures are being reshaped through network connections and multidimensional flows, especially in the contexts of urban-rural integration, metropolitan development, and coordinated regional growth.

We welcome submissions until **31 March 2027**. Topics and submission instructions are available on the [official special issue page](https://link.springer.com/collections/jicbecfhbh).
