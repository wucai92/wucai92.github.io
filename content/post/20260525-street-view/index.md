Our paper [*Beyond single snapshots: Quantifying multi-scale heterogeneity from street-view imagery and what it reveals about perception*]({{< relref "/publication/yang-2026-cities" >}}) is now available in *Cities*.

The study examines urban experience through sequences of street views, connecting visual variation across scales with how people perceive the city. It combines street-view imagery and geotagged social media data from Guangzhou.

Read the [publication details]({{< relref "/publication/yang-2026-cities" >}}), [publisher's version](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cities.2026.107227), or [studio announcement](https://urbanmorphology.studio/2026/05/25/beyond-single-snapshots.html).
