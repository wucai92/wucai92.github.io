Our paper [*Theory-informed and interpretable graph learning for urban commuting flows*]({{< relref "/publication/zhao-2026-scs" >}}) became available online in *Sustainable Cities and Society* on **13 June 2026**.

The paper introduces PIG-GNN, which brings geographic principles into graph learning for commuting-flow prediction. Using commuting data from England, the study examines how predictive accuracy and interpretable patterns of spatial interaction can be addressed together.
