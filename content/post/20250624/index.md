Urban Morphology Studio joined CUPUM 2025 in London for a rewarding week of workshops, presentations, and academic exchange.

During the conference, we contributed to discussions on AI and machine learning in urban planning, and shared ongoing work on GeoAI-based modelling of urban origin-destination flows.

The event was a meaningful opportunity to connect with colleagues in urban analytics and to strengthen the studio's international research network.
