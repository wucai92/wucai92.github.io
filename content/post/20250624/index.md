Urban Morphology Studio took part in [CUPUM 2025](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/bartlett/events/2025/jun/cupum-2025-19th-international-conference-computational-urban-planning-urban-management), held at UCL in London on **23–27 June 2025**.

The [paper on urban origin–destination flow prediction]({{< relref "/publication/zhao-2025-cupum" >}}) by **Minwei Zhao (first author) and Cai Wu** received the **Best Early Career Paper Award**. **Minwei is a PhD student supervised by Cai Wu.**

Cai Wu co-organized a workshop with Mingshu Wang and Ziqi Li on AI and machine learning in urban planning. The team's contributions also included Minwei's research on mobility prediction, Guosheng Yang's poster on street-view heterogeneity, and Yunlei Su's [work on VR-based urban perception]({{< relref "/publication/su-2025-cupum" >}}).
