Our paper [*Understanding multi-perspective urban green space patterns under urban expansion: Evidence from Guangzhou*]({{< relref "/publication/liu-2026-scs" >}}) is available in *Sustainable Cities and Society*.

The study combines satellite-based and street-level measures of greenery to examine urban expansion in Guangzhou. It shows why overhead vegetation coverage and greenery visible to pedestrians need to be considered together when assessing urban green space.

Read the [publication details]({{< relref "/publication/liu-2026-scs" >}}), [publisher's version](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scs.2026.107534), or [studio announcement](https://urbanmorphology.studio/2026/06/10/sustainable-cities-society-urban-green-space.html).
