I held a visiting fellowship at the University of Glasgow from **June to November 2025**, collaborating with Dr. Mingshu Wang.

Within this appointment, the **MASTS Visiting Fellowship Scheme supported a one-month research visit in June 2025**. This funded visit focused on the relationship between coastal urban activity and marine ecosystems, using literature analysis and spatial data.

See the [MASTS fellowship record](https://masts.ac.uk/news-events-opportunities/funding/visiting-fellowship-success/) and [project report](https://masts.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/MASTS-Visiting-Fellowship-final-report-VF90.pdf).
