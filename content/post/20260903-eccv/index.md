The camera-ready version of our [ECCV 2026 paper](https://eccv.ecva.net/Conferences/2026/AcceptedPapers), [*Urban Boundaries, Social Barriers: A Benchmark and Vision-Centric Framework for Mapping Gated Communities and Equity Implications*]({{< relref "/publication/zhao-2026-eccv" >}}), was released on arXiv on **3 September 2026**.

The study introduces a benchmark of residential compounds in China's Greater Bay Area and combines imagery, text, and structured information to identify gated communities. It also examines how community boundaries relate to green space and pedestrian connectivity.

Read the [publication details]({{< relref "/publication/zhao-2026-eccv" >}}) and [camera-ready paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03804).
